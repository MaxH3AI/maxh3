# Product reveal: ceramic cup

**Status:** original starting brief; not generation-tested. No output or performance claim is attached to this example.

## Goal

Reveal the glaze on one ceramic cup while keeping the object and background easy to inspect.

## Starting setup

Use a reference image you own, showing one cup on a simple tabletop. Select a supported image-to-video workflow and a short duration. Record the exact model and settings you choose.

## Prompt

```text
A single pale ceramic cup rests on a matte wooden table beside a window.
The cup stays still. The camera moves slowly closer, keeping the entire rim
visible. Soft window light reveals the uneven glaze without changing the
cup's shape. The background remains quiet and out of focus. End on a stable
three-quarter view of the cup. No hands, extra cups, or scene cuts.
```

## What to review

- Does the rim stay continuous from beginning to end?
- Does the cup keep its original handle and overall shape?
- Is the camera move readable without the object appearing to slide?
- Is the last frame stable enough to use as an ending?

If the shape changes, first try a smaller camera move. If the cup appears to slide, reinforce its stationary position while keeping the rest of the brief unchanged. These are hypotheses to test.
