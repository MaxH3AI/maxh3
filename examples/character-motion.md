# Character movement: a quiet glance

**Status:** original starting brief; not generation-tested. This is a planning exercise, not a verified model demonstration.

## Goal

Animate a small head turn from a prepared frame while preserving the character's overall appearance.

## Starting setup

Use your own character artwork or an authorized portrait. Choose a frame with a clear face and uncomplicated background. Select a supported image-to-video workflow and record its settings.

## Prompt

```text
The character stands beside a softly lit window. The camera remains locked.
They slowly turn their gaze toward the window, pause, and hold the final pose.
Their clothing and hairstyle remain consistent with the starting image.
Keep the movement gentle, the background still, and the face clearly visible.
One continuous shot, no scene change.
```

## What to review

- Do the eyes and facial features remain coherent during the turn?
- Does the movement stop in the requested final pose?
- Does the background remain stable?
- Does the result preserve the source's clothing and hairstyle?

If identity changes noticeably, try reducing the amount of head movement before adding more descriptive constraints. If the scene is too static, change one action instruction while keeping the source image and camera fixed.
